Site policy

Privacy Policy

PAL Privacy Guide is designed to collect as little information as practical.

Effective and last updated: 23 August 2026.

Information collected by this site

The published site source contains no account system, forms, comments embedded on the site, analytics, advertising tags, tracking pixels, social widgets, or non-essential cookies. PAL does not intentionally collect personal information through page visits.

Hosting infrastructure

This preview is intended to be hosted with GitHub Pages from the public xuanphucmk11-glitch/pal-privacy-guide repository. GitHub states that GitHub Pages is a static hosting service and that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub controls its own infrastructure and related processing under its applicable privacy terms. PAL will not enable optional analytics, advertising trackers, paid metered hosting features, or other tracking under the approved preview scope. Review GitHub's current privacy information at GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Corrections and privacy questions

The preview does not operate a contact form. For factual corrections, accessibility issues, or privacy questions, use the public GitHub Issues channel. Issues and comments are public and require a GitHub account to post. Information submitted there is also handled by GitHub under its own terms and privacy practices. Do not post passwords, payment details, identity documents, private addresses, account credentials, or other sensitive personal information.

Links to other sites

Links may lead to sites with their own privacy practices. A link is not an endorsement of those practices. Review the destination's policy before providing information.

Cookies and tracking choices

PAL Privacy Guide does not set cookies in this static source and does not add optional tracking. Because no optional PAL tracking is present, there is currently no PAL cookie-consent control. If site behavior changes, this policy and any required controls must be reviewed before publication.

Children

This general educational site is not directed to children and does not knowingly solicit personal information from them.

Changes

Material changes will be dated on this page. Deployment review must check that this policy matches the actual host and site behavior. Earlier versions remain available in project history.

Scope of this policy

This page describes the technical privacy practices of the PAL Privacy Guide preview as presently configured. It is not individualized legal advice and should not be read as a universal legal-compliance determination.

Questions

See About & Contact for the public corrections/contact channel and its limitations.
