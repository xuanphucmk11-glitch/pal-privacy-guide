Commercial transparency

Affiliate Disclosure

PAL Privacy Guide currently has no affiliate relationships, referral links, sponsorships, paid placements, or partner approvals.

Effective and last updated: 23 August 2026.

Current status

No link in this site source pays PAL a commission. The site does not claim to be a partner of Proton or any other vendor. Product names, if discussed in future editorial work, would identify the subject of reporting and would not imply endorsement or authorization.

If affiliate links are introduced later

They may be added only after a separate approval and compliance review. Any compensated link would be labeled clearly and close to the link, in language a reader can understand before clicking. The disclosure would explain that PAL may receive compensation if the reader completes a qualifying action, at no claim that price or eligibility is guaranteed.

A future commercial relationship would not allow a merchant to purchase a rating, pre-approve editorial conclusions, hide material limitations, or convert marketing claims into facts. Our evaluation method would remain applicable.

Paid search and traffic

No advertising campaign is active under this site-readiness work. If a compliant affiliate program and a separate advertising plan are later approved, intended traffic would go from an advertisement to relevant content on this independent site. It would not link an advertisement directly to a merchant referral URL. Merchant trademark bidding, ad copy, landing-page content, disclosures, and program terms would require review before launch.

How to interpret links

An ordinary external link is not automatically an affiliate link or endorsement. If compensation becomes possible, the specific page and link will say so. If a page does not contain a local disclosure, readers should not infer an undisclosed commission.
